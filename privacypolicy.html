StockFlow — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026 · Contact: shivansh.25mip10101@vitbhopal.ac.in

1. What StockFlow is

StockFlow is a shop management web application (stock tracking, GST billing, purchases, customer khata and related records) provided for your own store's internal use.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information via Google Sign-In: your name, email address and profile picture as provided by Google. We use it only to identify your account and store your shop records under it. We do not receive, read or store your Google password.
  • Email verification codes: if you use the optional email code login, the code we send is used only to verify that email address.
  • Shop data you enter: products, quantities, invoices, purchases, parties and payments you create. This is your data, stored on your behalf.
  • Technical data: standard web server logs (such as IP address, browser type) needed to operate the service.

3. How we use information

  • To authenticate you and keep your shop records securely associated with your login.
  • To sync your data across your own devices so you always see the latest stock and bills.
  • To provide the core features of the app (stock, billing, reports). We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Where your data is stored

Shop records and account links are stored in a cloud database (Supabase, hosted on AWS) protected by access rules. Your business data is visible only to your own logged-in account.

5. Data sharing

We share data only with authentication: Google (for sign-in identity) and our database provider (Supabase) for storage. No other third parties receive your data, and no data is sold.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your records are kept while your account is active. If you want your data deleted, contact us at the email above and we will remove your stored records. Logging out on a device only stops syncing; locally saved data may remain in that browser until you clear it.

7. Security

Login is handled by Google OAuth and Supabase Auth. Data is transmitted over HTTPS and protected by database-level row security.

8. Children's privacy

StockFlow is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when features change; the effective date above will be updated accordingly.
